Unique Experience

Where Comfort Meets the Spirit of Hunza

The Consorts Hotels & Resorts offers a thoughtfully crafted stay where modern comfort blends seamlessly with the natural charm of Hunza Valley. Surrounded by mountains, open skies, and a peaceful landscape, our resort is designed for guests seeking calm, warmth, and a genuine connection with nature.

Our innovative container-style accommodations reflect an eco-conscious approach, carefully positioned to respect the environment while offering inviting, cozy interiors. Each room features large windows that bring natural light and scenic mountain views indoors.

Beyond restful stays, we invite guests to experience Hunza at a slower, more meaningful pace. Wander through nearby villages, explore scenic trails, or simply unwind on your private balcony.
