Important Information

Booking Policies

Please review our policies before making your reservation

Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in time: 2:00 PM
  • Check-out time: 12:00 PM
  • Early check-in and late check-out available upon request
  • 24-hour front desk service

Payment & Deposits

The entire stay, including any additional charges, must be settled upon check-in. We accept cash, credit cards, and bank transfers as payment methods.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation/amendment within 48 hours is subject to a one-night charge. In case of a no-show, first-night room charges will apply. In case of unforeseen circumstances like landslides, road closures, or flight cancellations, payments are non-refundable but can be held as credit for future bookings (valid for 1 year).

Children & Extra Beds

2 Adults & 1 Child under 10 Years enjoy complimentary breakfast. Children aged 10–15: 50% breakfast charge. 15+ years: full breakfast + PKR 5,000 for room sharing. Extra mattress: PKR 1,500.
