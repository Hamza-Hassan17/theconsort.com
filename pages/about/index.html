About Us

Our story, vision, and commitment to sustainable luxury

Our Journey

The Consorts Hotels Story

The Consorts Hotels & Resorts is a premium hospitality destination in Hunza, offering a perfect blend of comfort, elegance, and personalized service. Designed for travelers who seek both relaxation and memorable experiences, our property features thoughtfully curated rooms, modern amenities, and breathtaking surroundings.

We take pride in delivering warm hospitality, exceptional service standards, and a peaceful retreat away from the everyday rush. Whether you’re visiting for leisure or a special getaway, The Consorts Hotels & Resorts ensures a stay defined by comfort, care, and unforgettable moments.

Our Philosophy

Vision & Values

At The Consorts Hotels, we are guided by a clear vision and a set of core values that shape everything we do.

Our Vision

To create extraordinary experiences that connect guests with the natural beauty of Hunza Valley while setting new standards for sustainable luxury hospitality.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional service and comfortable accommodations that showcase the unique beauty of Attabad Lake and Hunza Valley, while preserving the environment and supporting the local community.

Sustainability

We are committed to minimizing our environmental impact through innovative design, renewable energy, and responsible practices.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every aspect of our operations, from the quality of our accommodations to the service we provide.

Community

We are dedicated to supporting the local community through employment opportunities, sourcing local products, and cultural preservation.

Innovation

We embrace innovative solutions that enhance the guest experience while reducing our environmental footprint.

Eco-Friendly Approach

Our Commitment to Sustainability

At The Consorts Hotels, sustainability is not just a buzzword—it's at the core of everything we do. From our innovative container architecture to our daily operations, we are committed to minimizing our environmental impact while providing a luxury experience.
