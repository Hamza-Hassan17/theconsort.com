Location & Directions

The Consorts Hotels is located on the shores of Attabad Lake along the Karakoram Highway in Hunza Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan.

Open in Google Maps

How to Reach Us

By Air

The nearest airport is Gilgit Airport (GIL), approximately 120 km from the hotel. From Gilgit, you can arrange private transportation or take a local bus to Attabad Lake.

Alternatively, fly to Islamabad International Airport (ISB) and then travel by road to Hunza Valley.

By Road from Islamabad

The journey from Islamabad to The Consorts Hotels takes approx. 12–14 hours (600 km) via the Karakoram Highway (KKH).

  1. Take the Karakoram Highway (N-35) from Islamabad
  2. Pass through Abbottabad, Mansehra, and Besham
  3. Continue through Gilgit and Aliabad
  4. Proceed to Attabad Lake (~20 km past Aliabad)
  5. We’re on the eastern shore of the lake

By Public Transport

NATCO and other bus services run daily from Islamabad to Gilgit. From Gilgit, take a local van or bus to Attabad Lake. We recommend a pickup from Gilgit or Aliabad for convenience.

Hotel Transfer Service

We offer private transfer services from Gilgit Airport and Islamabad. Contact our reservations team 48 hours in advance to arrange.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about The Consorts Hotels.

Where are you located?

Main KKH, Near Attabad Boating Stand, Shishkat, Hunza, Gilgit Baltistan, Pakistan.

What is your reservation process?

You can make a reservation through booking platforms such as Booking.com, TripAdvisor, and Agoda. Alternatively, you can book directly through our website or contact us by phone.

What is check-in and check-out time?

Our check-in time is at 1:00 pm, and check-out is at 12:00 pm.

Do you have credit card facilities available?

Yes, we offer credit card facilities to ensure a seamless experience during your stay.

How long does it take from Gilgit to your hotel?

The journey from Gilgit to The Consorts Hotels & Resorts takes approximately 2–3 hours by car.

Should we land in Skardu or Gilgit?

It’s best to land in Gilgit, which is closer to Hunza. The drive from Gilgit takes 2–3 hours. If you land in Skardu, the journey would take 5–6 hours by road. We also provide transportation with pre-booking.
