Borith Lake

20 minutes (20 km)

Dating back over 700 years, Baltit Fort is an ancient fortress perched on a hill above Karimabad, the capital of Hunza Valley. This UNESCO World Heritage Site offers a fascinating glimpse into the region's history and the lives of the former rulers of Hunza.

Activities

  • Guided tours of the fort interior
  • Museum exhibits of local artifacts
  • Panoramic views of Hunza Valley
  • Photography of traditional architecture

Visitor Information

  • Open: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Tuesdays)
  • Entrance fee: PKR 500 for foreigners
  • Guided tours available in English

Insider Tip

Visit in the late afternoon when most tour groups have left. The light is perfect for photography, and you'll have a more intimate experience with the knowledgeable local guides who can share fascinating stories about the fort's history.

Shopping Destinations

Find authentic local crafts, souvenirs, and specialty food products

Karimabad Bazaar

Crafts, Textiles, Souvenirs Karimabad (25 min drive)

The main shopping area in Hunza Valley, offering a wide range of local crafts, textiles, and souvenirs. Look for hand-embroidered caps, walnut wood carvings, and gemstone jewelry.

Best buys: Hand-embroidered Hunza caps, walnut wood carvings, local gemstones

Women's Handicraft Center

Textiles, Embroidery Karimabad (25 min drive)

A cooperative supporting local women artisans, offering high-quality handmade textiles and embroidery. All purchases directly support the local women who create these beautiful items.

Best buys: Hand-embroidered shawls, traditional dresses, table linens

Hunza Food Products

Organic Food Products Aliabad (15 min drive)

Specializing in organic dried fruits, nuts, and local specialty foods from Hunza Valley. Their products are known for exceptional quality and are produced using traditional methods.

Best buys: Dried apricots, mulberries, walnuts, organic honey, apricot oil

Passu Carpet Workshop

Carpets, Rugs Passu (45 min drive)

A traditional carpet workshop where you can watch local artisans create beautiful hand-knotted carpets using ancient techniques. Each piece tells a story through its patterns and colors.

Best buys: Hand-knotted carpets, small rugs, wall hangings

Hidden Gems & Local Favorites

Discover lesser-known spots that offer authentic experiences away from the tourist crowds

Ondra Fort

Historical Site Gulmit (20 min drive)

While most tourists visit Baltit Fort, this lesser-known fortress offers a more authentic experience without the crowds. The caretaker often provides personal tours with fascinating historical insights.

Don't miss: The rooftop view of Gulmit village and surrounding mountains

Borith Lake

Natural Site Near Gulmit (30 min drive)

A serene high-altitude lake that's often overlooked by tourists. The lake is a birdwatcher's paradise and offers spectacular views of Passu Glacier. A small hike from the road leads to this tranquil spot.

Don't miss: Sunrise birdwatching, traditional tea at the lakeside café

Khunjerab Wildlife Sanctuary

Wildlife Viewing 2 hours drive

While many visitors go to Khunjerab Pass, few explore the wildlife sanctuary where you can spot rare species like the Marco Polo sheep, snow leopard, and Himalayan ibex with the help of local guides.

Don't miss: Early morning wildlife viewing, local guide insights

Traditional Music Evening

Cultural Experience Karimabad (25 min drive)

Every Friday evening, a local family hosts traditional music performances in their home. This authentic cultural experience includes traditional instruments, songs, and stories about Hunza heritage.

Don't miss: Learning to play traditional instruments, joining in the dancing

Year-Round Guide

Seasonal Recommendations

The best activities and experiences for each season in Hunza Valley

Spring (April - May)

Spring transforms Hunza Valley into a paradise of blossoms as the famous apricot, apple, and cherry trees burst into bloom, creating a spectacular display against the backdrop of snow-capped mountains.

Highlights:

  • Hunza Blossom Festival (late April)
  • Moderate temperatures perfect for hiking
  • Lush green landscapes with colorful wildflowers
  • Less crowded than summer months

Recommended Activities:

  • Photography tours of blooming orchards
  • Moderate hiking trails as they become accessible
  • Cultural festivals celebrating the new growing season
  • Bird watching as migratory species return

Summer (June - August)

Summer brings perfect weather conditions for exploring the highest peaks and passes of the region. This is the peak tourist season when all attractions and hiking trails are fully accessible.

Highlights:

  • Ideal weather for high-altitude exploration
  • Vibrant local festivals and polo matches
  • Fresh fruit harvest (cherries, mulberries, apricots)
  • Longest daylight hours for maximum exploration

Recommended Activities:

  • Trekking to high-altitude lakes and glaciers
  • Water activities on Attabad Lake
  • Camping under the stars
  • Jeep safaris to remote valleys
  • Traditional polo matches in Shandur (July)

Autumn (September - October)

Autumn paints Hunza Valley in spectacular shades of gold, amber, and crimson as the foliage changes color. The clear air offers the most dramatic mountain views of the year.

Highlights:

  • Breathtaking fall foliage
  • Harvest season for apples, grapes, and walnuts
  • Crystal clear views of mountain peaks
  • Pleasant temperatures and fewer tourists

Recommended Activities:

  • Photography tours of autumn landscapes
  • Harvest festivals and traditional food preparation
  • Moderate to challenging hikes with spectacular views
  • Fruit picking experiences at local orchards
  • Cultural experiences as communities prepare for winter

Winter (November - March)

Winter transforms Hunza Valley into a serene wonderland of snow-covered peaks and quiet villages. While some high-altitude areas become inaccessible, this season offers a unique, authentic experience of local life.

Highlights:

  • Snow-covered landscapes and dramatic scenery
  • Traditional winter festivals and celebrations
  • Authentic cultural experiences with few tourists
  • Cozy evenings by the fire with traditional music

Recommended Activities:

  • Winter photography of snow-capped mountains
  • Cultural immersion in local winter traditions
  • Snowshoeing and winter hiking on accessible trails
  • Traditional cooking classes featuring winter specialties
  • Stargazing in the crystal-clear winter night sky
